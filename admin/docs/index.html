Quick Start Guide

1

Login to Admin

Open the admin page and enter your credentials. If you don't have them, contact your system owner.

URL: app.sqms.com/admin
2

Select your Counter

Once logged in, your counter (Station 01, 02, etc.) will be automatically identified. Check the left sidebar to see all active counters.

3

Set Serving Service

Use the dropdown menu in the center to select which service you are serving today (e.g., General Inquiries, Technical Support).

Managing Counters

Counter Information

  • Active Counters: Numbers shown in the left list are currently online.
  • Status: Pulse animation means the counter is ready to call next.

Serving Customers

Action 1

Call Next

Click the large "CALL NEXT" button to bring the next person from the queue. The TV will announce their number.

Action 2

Complete

When finished, click "COMPLETE" to close the current token and prepare for the next.

Special Case

No Show?

If a customer doesn't arrive within 2 minutes, click the NO SHOW button. This will mark them as absent and remove them from the active display.

TV Display Setup

Syncing your TV

The TV display should be opened on a large screen in the waiting area. It stays in sync with all counters automatically.
